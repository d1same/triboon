# Source → playback review, 2026-10-07

Four read-only reviews (search/matching, health/warm-up, mount/bytes/picture,
failure recovery) plus a load simulation (`bench/multi-viewer-sim.js`) after the
Unraid incident (The Good Doctor freezes, a 4K start timing out at 0:05).

## Already fixed in 3.3.16 (see docs-streaming-performance.md "Allocation, Routing And Stealing")

- Auto under-sized 4K (4 lines): honest per-line speed, 1.5x headroom, startup ceiling,
  instant escalation under 8 s of buffer; speed test averages accounts by line count.
- Idle lines beside waiting work: routing prefers an idle open line; idle lines steal
  eligible fetches from other accounts' queues; stranded picture work is handed to an
  idle line (`dispatch stuck` log); richer `lines:` log.
- Cut-off / length-mismatched yEnc articles fail over instead of being zero-filled.
- Mid-play: a second failure within 20 s of media time (or 4 in 2 min) switches release.
- `playback-failed` survives a clean STAT; a second failure records `blocked`; a mount
  holding an unreadable piece is not re-joined.
- Search: S01E01E02(E03) files accepted; plain `.WEB.` source; `UPSCALED`; `DDP5.1`;
  partial fan-outs cached 60 s not 2 h; yearless query keeps numeric title words.

## Not fixed yet (ranked)

### High
1. **Prepare storm / priority inversion.** `vfs.readAt` defaults to `startup`, so a
   background prepare's mount header reads outrank the active viewer's `playback`
   pieces. ~15 prepares ran at once (20 s each) beside one stream. Cap concurrent
   prepares to 1-2, abort at `PREPARE_MAX_MS`, cancel on client disconnect, pause
   prepares while another stream is active, read prepare headers at `health` lane.
   (pipeline.js StartupGate / `_tryCandidate`, vfs.js:697)
2. **Native retry loop** (MainActivity.java:4638-4660, 6302): 6 retries per 90 s window
   that resets, so a source failing every 15 s never reaches JS. Needs a failure count
   with last position surviving window resets, and a structured `{code, repeated}` error.
   The web side (3.3.16) now escalates, but native silent retries still delay it.
3. **Health is STAT-only** (vfs.js triage, archive.js): ~0.2 % coverage of a 2,900-article
   set; never reads bytes. Add a tiny BODY sample (head, tail window, first article of each
   RAR volume), re-probe on any miss, `blocked` on 2+ misses, par2 `hash16k` volume check.
4. **`probeFirstArticle` false `missing`** (pipeline.js:3118, nntp.js `_parallelStat`): with
   no logins up it asks ONE provider; a 430 from a short-retention account records a
   release-wide 6 h `missing`. Ask >= 2 providers; unknown errors are `error`, not `missing`.
5. **Dead pieces never reach the verdict cache** (vfs `_noteDeadPiece`): a fresh mount of
   the same NZB passes the STAT gate again.

### Medium
6. Search: extra jobs start only AFTER the main query (cold Play = main + ~3 rounds of
   extras); empty searches are never cached and re-run a second fan-out (stale retry).
7. Search: no `cat` filter, no page 2; `usage.onSearch` counts hits for cooling indexers;
   quota/auth errors cool an indexer only 60 s; merged extras are not de-duplicated.
8. Title matching: foreign/romanised/AKA titles (trust a positive IMDb/TVDB tag over the
   title anchor); `Dr. Strangelove or: ...`; `Twelve Monkeys` vs `12.Monkeys`; `Alien³`;
   hard year reject for old films (Criterion years differ >1); season ranges / complete
   series packs; date-based daily shows; `minsize=1500MB` on widen jobs hides SD classics.
9. Scoring: pre-release tiers (SCR/R5/PPV/WORKPRINT/HDRip) score above DVDRip; `Cam` in a
   title word is read as a cam source; `The.Danish.Girl` gets `foreign-dub -800`; episodes
   with an air-year tag get `wrong year -250`; 2160p AVC under ~4 GB per 100 min not flagged.
10. Mount: flat video next to `Subs.rar` mounts the archive and `pickInner` returns junk;
    `.iso/.vob/VIDEO_TS/BDMV` not rejected after mount; duplicate/missing RAR volumes;
    ZIP64 / `.z01` splits; HEVC remux lacks `-tag:v hvc1`; VAAPI probe args differ from the
    real transcode; `detectEncoder()` blocks the event loop on first Play; HDR tone-map runs
    before the downscale; HEAD on `/api/remux|transcode|hls` spawns ffmpeg.
11. Client: native stall watchdog waits 30 s (8 s with an empty buffer should call JS);
    "Try again" replays the same pinned source; `/api/play` 20/min lockout can trip on a
    retry storm; `/api/playback-issue` is log-only (carry code/position/count).
12. Two readers on one segment can serve wrong bytes (vfs.js:542-546, double skip);
    per-article `=ypart` offsets are not validated against the grid after segment 0.

### Low
13. Cache eviction thrash on short back-skips; no cross-volume read-ahead; disk-cache index
    rewritten on every flush; yEnc CRC could use `zlib.crc32`; one ffmpeg per user per mount;
    no `-rw_timeout` on the remux input; `catalogFactsFor` memoises failures forever.

## Investigated and found NOT a problem
- "Stream handler ends cleanly short and the player waits": on this Node version the
  connection is dropped at once (pinned by an e2e test).
- Range parsing, RAR4/RAR5 header walking, par2 obfuscated-volume rename, ffmpeg cleanup on
  client disconnect, A/V sync flags.
